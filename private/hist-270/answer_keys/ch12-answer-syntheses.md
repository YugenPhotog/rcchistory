Source: `public/hist-270/background_essays/essays/ch12/ch12-l1-mongol-conquest-state-building.html`

Context (nearest preceding heading): h3 — The human cost

Plain text:

Answer synthesis · Q1 Chinggis built a new kind of steppe army from decimal units and a guard bound to him personally. He used it first to make Xia a vassal and then to break Jin in the field. For years the Mongols treated North China mainly as a place to plunder, handing out fiefs and leaving local strongmen in charge, though overseers and Muqali's command show them beginning to hold territory from 1214–1217 onward. Military success came at an enormous human cost.

## Ranks, Registers and Roads: How the Yuan Sorted Its People and Opened Eurasia

Source: `public/hist-270/background_essays/essays/ch12/ch12-l2-ranks-registers-and-roads.html`

### 1. Answer synthesis · Q5

Context (nearest preceding heading): h3 — One script for the state

Plain text:

Answer synthesis · Q5 The four labels served Mongol interests by reserving the top posts and the overseer's seal for Mongols, using semu experts as a counterweight to Chinese officials, and dividing northern Hanren from the larger and richer Nanren. Rules on weapons, assembly, names and punishment kept the Chinese disarmed and legally inferior. Hereditary registers guaranteed soldiers, craftsmen, salt and relay service without bargaining with each generation. The system was harsh and real, but it was applied unevenly. Many Chinese rose within it, and its most famous slogan, the "ten grades," was a loyalist's bitter joke.

### 2. Answer synthesis · Q8

Context (nearest preceding heading): h3 — Astronomy, medicine and history

Plain text:

Answer synthesis · Q8 Mongol rule linked China and Iran under related khans and protected the merchant networks between them. Those networks carried cobalt east to Jingdezhen, where a porcelain bureau of 1278 and an old craft tradition turned it into a mature underglaze style, and they carried finished wares west to buyers whose tastes shaped size and decoration. Quanzhou, the canal and the sea route supplied the infrastructure. The David Vases of 1351 show that the style was fully formed under the Yuan and used at home as well as abroad. The same networks moved astronomy, medicine and history, so porcelain is the clearest case of a wider exchange.

### 1. Answer synthesis · Q6

Context (nearest preceding heading): h3 — Zhao Mengfu and the collaborator debate

Plain text:

Answer synthesis · Q6 The conquest ended the examinations for forty years, filled offices with Mongols, semu and clerks, and so broke the link between classical learning and office that had defined the Song elite. Scholars responded in several ways: loyalists like Wen Tianxiang and Xie Fangde refused to serve; many more became teachers, clerks, doctors, clergy, playwrights and painters, or led their localities; and some, like Zhao Mengfu, served the Yuan to influence it, at a cost to their reputations.

### 2. Answer synthesis · Q7

Context (nearest preceding heading): h3 — Quotas and two lists

Plain text:

Answer synthesis · Q7 The revival of 1313–1315 was real and lasting in one way: it made Zhu Xi's Neo-Confucianism the standard of Chinese examinations for six centuries. As a road to office it stayed narrow. Equal quotas for unequal groups, easier papers for Mongols and semu , separate lists and regional limits reproduced the Yuan hierarchy inside the examination hall, and graduates filled only a few percent of posts. Bayan's suspension in 1335 shows how fragile even that was.

### 3. Answer synthesis · Q9

Context (nearest preceding heading): h3 — The Western Chamber

Plain text:

Answer synthesis · Q9 Drama flourished because a living tradition of performance met a supply of educated men with time, skill and few careers, and because rich cities and temple festivals provided audiences and patrons. Literati playwrights raised the literary quality of scripts, gave heroines like Dou E a voice against injustice and corrupt officials, and played with the values of the examination world. Restricted careers were one cause, not the only one, and most texts reach us in Ming revisions.

### 4. Answer synthesis · Q10

Context (nearest preceding heading): h3 — The Four Masters

Plain text:

Answer synthesis · Q10 Under the Yuan, leadership in painting passed from court and professional painters to scholars. They revived older styles, joined poem, calligraphy and image, and painted for friends rather than patrons. For men without office, painting offered a respected occupation, a circle of like-minded friends and a private language for values the times denied them: integrity, retirement and loyalty to the past. Political meanings were sometimes there, but they are interpretations, not facts on the silk.

### 5. Answer synthesis · Q11

Context (nearest preceding heading): h3 — Zhu Yuanzhang

Plain text:

Answer synthesis · Q11 The Yuan fell to a combination. Succession feuds after 1307 left a divided court ruled by strongmen. Overprinting of paper money, made worse by war, destroyed trust in the currency. The flood of 1344 and the huge repair of 1351 ruined and mobilized the Huai region. The Red Turbans and rival warlords turned those crises into a collapse, and the dismissal of Toghto at Gaoyou in 1355 threw away the court's last chance. No single cause was enough. Together they let Zhu Yuanzhang take China.
