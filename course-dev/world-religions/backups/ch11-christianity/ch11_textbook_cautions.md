# Ch11 Christianity: textbook cautions (instructor notes, not for the decks)

Prepared 2026-10-19 during the Ch11 revision pass.

1. **Vatican II dates.** The reading guide (Q6) says "the textbook dates it from 1962 to 1966." The Second Vatican Council met from 1962 to **1965** (it closed December 8, 1965). The Rabbit Hole supplement already uses 1962–1965. If students quote 1966 from the textbook, accept the anchor sentence but correct the date in discussion.
2. **Earliest Church dates.** The textbook frames the "Earliest Church" period as 30–100 CE, while L1's original slide said 33–90. Both are conventions. The decks keep the slide's 33–90 framing; if students cite the textbook's 30–100, accept it.
3. **"Tolerated in 312."** The textbook ties toleration to 312 (the Milvian Bridge). The Constantine–Licinius agreement on toleration (the "Edict of Milan") is dated 313 and was posted at Nicomedia in June 313 (Lactantius, *Deaths of the Persecutors* 48). Decks and the Persecution to Nicaea mini-lecture use 313.
4. **Duplicate question numbers.** The reading guide has two Question 3s and two Question 5s; pause slides cite the section title to disambiguate.
