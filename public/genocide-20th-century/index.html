About This Lecture Arc

Man's Inhumanity to Man

This lecture arc for PHIL 210 World Religions examines genocide and crimes against humanity in the twentieth century. It begins with the Holocaust, widens to the century of genocide, and closes with the problem of human evil. The lecture materials are listed below.
