The Problem of Human Evil

God, evil, and human choice after the Holocaust

A hanging at Buna opens the question. Jewish and Christian thinkers answer it differently; Jedwabne and Le Chambon-sur-Lignon show what ordinary people chose; Kant’s radical evil and Arendt’s banality of evil give us two ways to name what we have seen.
