Genocide Resources

Trusted museums, memorials, archives, and institutions for learning more about the genocides and mass atrocities in this lecture. Each link opens in a new tab.

  • USHMM Simon-Skjodt Center for the Prevention of GenocideThe U.S. Holocaust Memorial Museum's center for research, early warning, and education on preventing genocide and mass atrocities. (opens in a new tab)
  • UN Office on Genocide Prevention and the Responsibility to ProtectThe United Nations office that monitors the risk of genocide and explains the Genocide Convention and the Responsibility to Protect. (opens in a new tab)
  • Armenian Genocide Museum-InstituteThe museum and research institute at the Tsitsernakaberd memorial in Yerevan, Armenia, with documents, photographs, and testimonies. (opens in a new tab)
  • Holodomor Research and Education Consortium (HREC)A research and education center at the University of Alberta's Canadian Institute of Ukrainian Studies, with lessons, maps, and sources on the 1932–1933 famine. (opens in a new tab)
  • Gulag: Many Days, Many LivesAn online exhibit on the Soviet Gulag from George Mason University's Roy Rosenzweig Center for History and New Media, with archival documents and prisoner memoirs. (opens in a new tab)
  • The Nanking Massacre Project (Yale Divinity Library)Letters, diaries, films, and photographs of Western eyewitnesses to the Nanjing massacre of 1937–1938, including Minnie Vautrin and John Magee. (opens in a new tab)
  • Documentation Center of Cambodia (DC-Cam)Cambodia's leading archive and research center on the Khmer Rouge regime, holding records, interviews, and maps of mass graves. (opens in a new tab)
  • Tuol Sleng Genocide MuseumThe museum at the former S-21 prison in Phnom Penh, Cambodia, whose archive is listed in UNESCO's Memory of the World Register. (opens in a new tab)
  • Kigali Genocide MemorialThe memorial in Kigali, Rwanda, where more than 250,000 victims of the 1994 genocide against the Tutsi are buried, with exhibitions and education programs. (opens in a new tab)
  • Srebrenica Memorial CenterThe memorial and cemetery at Potočari, Bosnia and Herzegovina, honoring the victims of the July 1995 genocide, with research and testimonies. (opens in a new tab)
  • UN International Residual Mechanism for Criminal TribunalsContinues the work of the Rwanda and former Yugoslavia tribunals (ICTR and ICTY) and holds their case records and judgments. (opens in a new tab)
