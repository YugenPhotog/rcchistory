Further Study

Holocaust Resources

Trusted museums, archives, and memorials for learning more about the Holocaust. Each link opens in a new tab.

  • United States Holocaust Memorial Museum↗The national Holocaust museum in Washington, DC, with exhibitions, collections, and learning resources. (opens in a new tab)
  • USHMM Holocaust Encyclopedia↗Short, reliable articles, maps, photographs, and timelines on the Holocaust, in many languages. (opens in a new tab)
  • Yad Vashem↗Israel's World Holocaust Remembrance Center in Jerusalem, with research, education, and online exhibitions. (opens in a new tab)
  • Yad Vashem Central Database of Shoah Victims' Names↗A searchable record of millions of Jews murdered in the Holocaust, drawn from testimonies and documents. (opens in a new tab)
  • Auschwitz-Birkenau Memorial and Museum↗The official memorial at the site of the former camp in Oświęcim, Poland, with history and visitor information. (opens in a new tab)
  • USC Shoah Foundation↗Founded to record video testimonies of Holocaust survivors and witnesses, now home to a large testimony archive. (opens in a new tab)
  • Illinois Holocaust Museum & Education Center↗A museum in Skokie, Illinois, devoted to Holocaust remembrance and education for Illinois students. (opens in a new tab)
  • Arolsen Archives↗The international center on Nazi persecution, holding millions of original documents about victims and survivors. (opens in a new tab)
  • Fortunoff Video Archive for Holocaust Testimonies↗Yale University's collection of videotaped interviews with Holocaust survivors and witnesses. (opens in a new tab)
  • The Holocaust Explained (Wiener Holocaust Library)↗A student-friendly guide to the Holocaust created by the Wiener Holocaust Library in London. (opens in a new tab)
