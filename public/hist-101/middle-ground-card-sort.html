Historical context & sources

These cards are teaching scenarios adapted from themes in “The Republics,” section one. They are not quotations or transcripts of specific councils. Cards 5–6 make the pressures described in the essay concrete; they do not imply that every community experienced the same conditions.

Richard White, The Middle Ground: Indians, Empires, and Republics in the Great Lakes Region, 1650–1815 (1991; twentieth-anniversary edition, 2011). Read the publisher's introduction (PDF) ↗. White's framework concerns historically specific relationships of accommodation and mutual need; it does not imply equality, harmony, or one unified Native policy.
