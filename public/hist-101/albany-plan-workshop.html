Historical context & scoring

This activity is based on section 4 of the course background essay, “The Republics.” The matching exercise simplifies a larger political debate into four distinct best-fit concerns. The final question restores the plural explanation: identity mattered, but institutional power, provincial interests, differing assessments of danger, and imperial policy also shaped the result.

The Albany Congress adopted an amended plan on July 10, 1754. No colonial assembly subsequently adopted it, and British authorities did not enact it.
