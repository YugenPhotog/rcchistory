## Production method and source authority

Each notebook was built lecture by lecture from the approved professor background essay and the final Reveal deck. The essay governed historical accuracy, interpretation, evidence, and nuance; the deck governed classroom sequence, student-facing wording, authentic activities, and pacing. The finalized Chapter 2 notebook supplied the visual and technical model.

Before drafting each chapter, a coverage matrix matched every major lecture movement to at least one note cue, authentic activity, closing takeaway, retrieval question, or learning check. The matrices are summarized below. No activity was invented to satisfy an activity count.

Count definitions used in this report:

- **Activities found/included** counts distinct deck activities. A single activity that returns later for reconsideration still counts once.
- **Activity response fields** counts one textarea or one radio-button group as one response unit and each ranking selector as one response unit.
- **Optional-review fields** includes all organizer inputs, retrieval and synthesis responses, the Remaining Question field, and learning-check checkboxes. Each notebook has 16 optional-review fields, including exactly six review textareas.
