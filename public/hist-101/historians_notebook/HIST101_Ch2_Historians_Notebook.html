Chapter Inquiry

The question this chapter answers

Why did one nation's colonizing ventures produce several different Americas — and no single authority over any of them?

Lecture 1 argues that colonization happened where separate motives converged, not because England followed a plan. Lectures 2 and 3 show what happened next: goals, environments, migration streams, and labor systems turned English settlers into genuinely different colonial societies. Lecture 4 then asks who had the right to govern the result — and finds several claimants and no referee.
