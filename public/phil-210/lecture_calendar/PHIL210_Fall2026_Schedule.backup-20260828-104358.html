Course Rhythm

Each major religion unit follows a consistent documentary-first, sacred-text-last sequence:

Hinduism
Documentary → 6 lectures → Bhagavad Gita
Buddhism
Documentary → lectures + Life of the Buddha → Dhammapada
Chinese Traditions
Documentary → lectures + Confucius: Words of Wisdom → Sacred Text workshop
Judaism
Documentary → 6 lectures → Hebrew Bible / Rabbinic Wisdom
Christianity
Your introductory video → 8 lectures → New Testament / Nicene Creed
Islam
Documentary → 6 lectures → Qur'an

The Nov. 12 transition from Christianity into Islam is the only place where the next religion begins before the previous religion's Canvas coursework is formally due, but it is a clean transition: Christianity's classroom instruction ends Nov. 10, its work is due Nov. 13, and Islam begins with the documentary on Nov. 12. This avoids compressing Islam because of Thanksgiving while preserving the complete documentary-first / sacred-text-last architecture.
