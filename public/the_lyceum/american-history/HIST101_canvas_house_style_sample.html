Gold Buttons Belong on Dark Bands

A gold fill on this dark teal band passes. The same gold fill on white does not. If a link opens in a new tab, say so in the visible text, and omit decorative play symbols that a screen reader might announce.

As You Go Through This Unit, Watch For…

  • A short label. One or two sentences of guidance in cream text, with the label in gold.
  • A second label. Gold at 17px on black already passes 4.5:1, so these labels do not need a larger size.
  • Who the work is for. Write for first-year students, in plain language, with no em dashes.
