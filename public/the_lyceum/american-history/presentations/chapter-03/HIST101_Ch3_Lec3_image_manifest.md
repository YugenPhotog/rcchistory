## Summary for delivery report

- Essential question: Does the evidence actually support the claim that the Salem crisis was shaped by the frontier Indian war — and how much weight can that single cause bear?
- Narrative architecture: **Claim-and-evidence "courtroom"** (state Norton's thesis openly → two witnesses for the claim: Lewis, Burroughs → cross-examination / the honest objection → a verdict that is a *verb*, "shaped by" not "caused by" → motive: why fear turned inward). Deliberately distinct from L1 (reverse-mystery) and L2 (source-pivot): because the assigned film already delivers the narrative, the deck weighs an argument rather than solving a mystery or auditing a source.
- **Video-dependent by design.** The deck opens on "You already know what happened" and uses VIDEO TIE-IN panels to point back to specific moments students watched (York in flames, Mercy Lewis's arc, Burroughs at the gallows, Mather's "Sagamores" line, the trials collapsing). Requires the *Witch Hunt* screening to be due before class — flagged in the essay and in persistent memory.

- Independent Study slides: 4 (1 divider + 3 content: spectral evidence on trial, the Putnam feud, how the crisis ended). Note: IS here deepens *analysis* rather than housing overflow narrative, because the film already carries the story.
- Pop-ups: 3 content (Wabanaki, Putnam faction, spectral evidence) + 1 keyboard utility.
