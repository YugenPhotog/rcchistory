## Notes on image selection

This deck uses 3 major visuals — fewer than L1's 4 and within the skill's descriptive range for a lecture where argument density (four converging claims, a textbook correction, a historiographical caution on the Albany Plan) competes for classroom time against visual dwell time. The convergence architecture is carried primarily through the `.claim-card` grid component (slide 9) rather than through additional imagery, which keeps the valley's political complexity legible without overloading the visual channel.

No portrait of a valley resident (Delaware, Shawnee, or Mingo leader) from this specific period and place was located and verified in time for this build. This is a genuine gap — the deck currently visualizes the converging claims (Hendrik, Franklin, the French map) more fully than the valley's own communities. Flagging this for a future revision pass: a verified image of a Logstown-adjacent site, or a securely attributed period image of a Delaware or Shawnee leader from the 1740s–50s, would strengthen the deck's balance and is worth a dedicated search before the next classroom use.
