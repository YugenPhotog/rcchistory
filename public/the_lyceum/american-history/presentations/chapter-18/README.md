## The shared deck and the shortcut

Lecture 2A is the same deck as Chapter 13, Lecture 2. The real deck, with its images, audio, and captions,
lives in `../chapter-13/`. `ch18-lecture2A-picturing_manifest_destiny.html` here is only a forwarding page:
it opens the chapter-13 deck right away (meta refresh plus a script that keeps any `#/slide` hash) and shows
a fallback link. Old links and bookmarks to the 2A file name keep working. Edit the deck in chapter-13, not here.

The Mythic West (Lecture 2) links to 2A twice: the "Launch Picturing Manifest Destiny Lecture" slide and the
Part IV divider. Both point at the forwarding page.

For what the shared deck does (Audio Guide, captions, Study Buddy, Look Closer, the seven typed reflections
with one .txt download on the last slide), see `../chapter-13/README.md`.
