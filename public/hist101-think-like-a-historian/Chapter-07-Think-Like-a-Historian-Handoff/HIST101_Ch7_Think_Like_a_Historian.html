  1. ExplainWeigh plausible historical explanations.
  2. UseTest an interpretation against new evidence.
  3. ConnectBuild a larger interpretation from more than one lecture.

New for Exam 2: Which explanation is stronger?

More than one interpretation can contain part of the truth. Your job is to ask which one has greater explanatory power.

  • What does each interpretation explain well?
  • What evidence creates trouble for it?
  • Which interpretation explains more evidence with fewer exceptions?
  • Could the weaker interpretation still be partly right?
