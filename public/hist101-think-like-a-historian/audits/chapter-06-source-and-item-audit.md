## Historical cautions preserved

- Military victory is distinguished from political control and allegiance.
- British and Patriot freedom offers are selective and strategic, not universal emancipation.
- Native nations make distinct strategic choices; the Treaty of Paris does not erase their sovereignty.
- The Confederation is evaluated as an experiment with achievements and limits, not a caricature.
- Shays's Rebellion is consequential evidence in a contested crisis argument, not a self-explaining verdict.
