## Historical cautions preserved

- Colonization is explained by convergence and increasing capacity, not one motive or an inevitable Armada-to-Jamestown chain.
- Racial slavery is treated as a constructed process, not a complete system created at one moment.
- Puritan freedom is distinguished from broad toleration; representation is distinguished from modern democracy.
- Native nations and Tsenacommacah remain political worlds with sovereignty and diplomacy.
