# HIST 101 Practice Source Map: Chapters 2–6

## Selection method

The files below were selected by chapter number, HIST 101 course prefix, lecture sequence, revision label, and agreement with each chapter's Historian's Notebook. Background essays control factual and interpretive qualification; Reveal decks control student-facing emphasis; notebooks identify retention and synthesis expectations. Image manifests, archives, HIST 102 materials, and unrelated presentation folders were excluded.

## Boundary decisions

- Practice questions use no direct quotation except where a stem asks how to evaluate a phrase already foregrounded in the approved lecture; no item asks students to authenticate a reconstructed quote.
- Precise casualty and population totals were avoided when they were unnecessary to the reasoning task or contested in the background essays.
- Native nations, Black freedom seekers, Acadians, colonists, and imperial officials are treated as distinct actors rather than collapsed into binary sides.
- No secure exam files were opened or reused. Concept overlap is permitted; wording, choices, scenarios, and source combinations were independently authored for practice.
