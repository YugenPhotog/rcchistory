## Historical cautions preserved

- Metacom is not treated as absolute commander of a unified Native side.
- Contested casualties and source wording are not turned into false precision.
- Pueblo testimony is identified as coerced, translated, and filtered without being discarded.
- Frontier war is a strong Salem interpretation within a multicausal crisis, not a sole cause.
- Captive choices support bounded inference about belonging; archival silence is not filled with invented quotation.
