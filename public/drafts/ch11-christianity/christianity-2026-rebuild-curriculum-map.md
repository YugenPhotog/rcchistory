- Three 70-minute class meetings, each containing two separate 35-minute Reveal.js decks (210 classroom minutes total).
- **Day 1 (70 minutes)** is dedicated to the life, teachings, miracles, identity, death and resurrection of Jesus of Nazareth.
- Short video clips are used for pacing, with additional linked clips reserved for independent viewing, not automatically played in class.
- **Pierian Spring is required independent study** and not an optional extension or extra-credit item. Its main deck also assigns the linked `Global, Modern & Alternative Traditions` mini-lecture as required study.
- The existing Christianity Sacred Text Packet and its associated chatbot remain separate **homework**; no 35-minute primary-source classroom block is reserved.
- Existing published Lyceum files are not touched.

## Historical and theological guardrails

- Jesus' teachings and debates are located within **Second Temple Judaism**; do not imply a simplistic 'Jewish legalism vs Christian love' contrast.
- The Gospels convey theological literary portrayals. Historians can investigate attested traditions without claiming to prove miracles or resurrection supernaturally.
- The four Gospels do not use identical claims about Jesus' identity; distinguish Synoptic accounts from John's explicit 'I AM' language.
- Locate responsibility for Jesus' Roman execution specifically. Avoid collective blame of Jewish people, which has contributed to antisemitism.
- Distinguish Constantine's toleration and patronage from Theodosius I's state religious policy later in the fourth century.
- Do not treat 'heresy', Miaphysite/Monophysite labels, or later confessional categories as neutral or timeless.
- Christianity outside western Europe has roots from antiquity, not only modern missions.
